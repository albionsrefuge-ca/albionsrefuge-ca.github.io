    1910.04.21 emigrated to Canada Travelled from Liverpool, England on the Allen Line's S.S. Corsican bound for Montreal Quebec and Quebec, Quebec. From Library and Archives Canada, Reference: RG 76, Microfilm: T-4765: ship's passenger list - they are on page 27. On their ticket: Walter Bester (34) a farmer, Alice Bester (35) and Walter Bester (11). Their destination is Danville, Quebec, they had $200.00 in hand. They are stamped G.T.R. and British Bonus Allowed. Also on board was a Hy Bester (21) butcher/farmer - could this be brother Harry?
